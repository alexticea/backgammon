# Privacy Policy for Backgammon Solana

**Last Updated: March 3, 2026**

Backgammon Solana ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you use our application.

### 1. Information Collection
Backgammon Solana is a decentralized application built on the Solana blockchain.
- **Public Blockchain Data**: We interact with public blockchain data including your public wallet address.
- **Game Data**: We store game-related statistics (wins, losses, XP, and rank) and custom display names on our secure servers to provide the leaderboard and multiplayer functionality.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiable information (PII).

### 2. Data Usage
The info we collect is used solely to:
- Maintain the global player leaderboard.
- Enable real-time multiplayer invitations and gameplay.
- Synchronize your game profile across different devices using your public wallet address.

### 3. Data Protection
Your display name and game stats are stored in a secure database. Your wallet's private keys are never requested or stored by our application; all transactions and connections are handled through recognized Solana wallet providers (e.g., Solflare).

### 4. Third-Party Services
We use official Solana wallet adapters and RPC providers to interact with the blockchain. These services have their own privacy policies.

### 5. Contact
If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
